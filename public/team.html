Our principles

How we work

Owners first

We only assist the legitimate owner of an account or page, and we say no to everything else.

People, not just bots

The AI assistant answers instantly, and real team members handle every case.

Reported openly

Team results are submitted by members, approved by admins and shown live on the home page.

Roles explained

Who does what

Everyone on the team has a clear role, so cases and results stay organized and honest.

Founder

Leads the team, sets the standards for how cases are handled and keeps the service accountable.

Admins

Review member reports, support the team and keep the platform running properly.

Members

Work on cases with owners, keep them updated and submit reports of completed work.

Our commitments

What we stand for

  • Honest communication about what is realistic
  • Your details are used only to work on your case
  • We only help the legitimate owner of an account or page
  • We never ask for passwords, login codes or ID documents in chat
  • We do not guarantee outcomes, because the platform decides
Transparency

How results reach this website

  • A team member submits a report of a completed case
  • An admin reviews member reports before they are published
  • Approved reports are added to the totals
  • The latest approved reports show on the home page dashboard
