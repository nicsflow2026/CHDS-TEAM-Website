FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Will CHDS Support ever ask for my password?

No. CHDS Support will never ask you to share your account password. Never send your password through AI Support, Human Support, Telegram, chat messages, or any support form. Keep your password private.

Getting started

Who can you help?

Only the rightful owner of an account or page. We ask you to show the account is yours, and we do not assist with accounts or pages that belong to someone else.

How do I start?

Tap Get help and describe what happened, or open the chat. Our AI assistant collects the key details instantly.

What information do you need from me?

The account or page affected, what happened and when, whether you still have access to the linked email or phone, and any notices or screenshots you received. We will tell you what else your case needs.

How much does recovery cost?

Pricing depends on the case, so we do not list fixed prices. Ask our team for a quote through the chat or on Telegram.

Recovery

How long does recovery take?

It depends on the platform's review. Some cases resolve in days, others take longer. We keep you updated while we work.

Do you guarantee recovery?

No one can guarantee it. The platform makes the final decision. We maximize your chances with a correct, complete case.

My account was hacked. What should I do first?

Secure your email account first, stop using the same password anywhere else, and warn friends if the hacker is messaging them. Save every notice, email and screenshot you received, then contact us.

My account was disabled. Can it be restored?

Sometimes. It depends on the reason and on the platform's review. We help you understand what happened and prepare an accurate case, but the outcome is decided by the platform.

I lost access to my email or phone number. Can you still help?

Possibly. It depends on what you still have access to and what the account history shows. Tell us exactly what you have and what you lost, and we will review your case.

Someone is impersonating me. Can you help?

Yes, we can help you document the fake profile or Page and pursue its removal. Send us the link, screenshots and the date you noticed it.

Situations and account types

What is the difference between hacked, disabled and locked out?

Hacked means someone else controls the account or changed its details. Disabled means the platform turned it off after a policy or security decision. Locked out means the account is yours and unchanged, but you cannot sign in, for example after losing a device or 2FA codes. Each has a different route, so describing yours accurately helps.

Can a deleted account be recovered?

It depends on how and when it was deleted. Platforms usually allow only a limited window after a deletion request, and after that an account generally cannot be restored. Tell us when it happened and we will review what is realistic.

I am no longer the admin of my Page. Can you help?

If you are the rightful owner, yes. We review how the Page roles were set up, what you still have access to and what happened, then prepare your case.

Can you help with a restricted or disabled ad account?

Yes. We review the notice with you and help prepare an accurate appeal. The platform makes the final decision.

Can you get me into someone else's account?

No. We only assist the rightful owner of an account or page, and we say no to everything else.

The verification code never arrives. What can I check?

Check the number or email on the account, your spam folder, your signal or internet connection, and try a device you have used before. If you are still stuck, contact us with what you see on screen.

Should I create a new account while I wait?

You can, but keep notes and screenshots of the original account and avoid creating many accounts, since that can conflict with platform rules.
