Warning signs

Is your account compromised?

  • A login or password-change email you did not request
  • Your email, phone number, name or username changed
  • Posts, messages or ads you did not create
  • Logins from devices or places you do not recognize
  • Friends say you are sending them strange links
  • Your correct password suddenly stops working
First steps

What to do right away

  • Secure your email account first
  • Stop reusing the same password anywhere else
  • Save every notice, email and screenshot you receive
  • Warn friends if the account is messaging them
  • Do not pay anyone who guarantees recovery
  • Contact us only through the channels on this website
