Understand your situation

Hacked, disabled or locked out?

The words sound similar, but each situation has a different route. Knowing which one you are in helps us help you faster.

Hacked

Someone else controls the account or changed your email, phone or password. Time and a secure email account matter most.

Disabled or suspended

The platform turned the account off after a policy or security decision. The route is a review with an accurate case.

Locked out

The account is yours and unchanged, but you cannot sign in because of a lost device, 2FA code or old email.

Impersonation

A fake profile or Page uses your name, photos or brand. We help you document it and pursue removal.

Our limits

What we never do

  • Ask for your password, login codes or ID documents in chat
  • Guarantee recovery or a timeline: the platform decides
  • Work on accounts or pages that are not yours
  • Share your case with people outside the team handling it
After recovery

Keep your account safe

A few habits make it much harder to lose access again.

Use an authenticator app

Turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app instead of relying only on SMS.

Use a unique password

Never reuse a password. A password manager makes long, unique passwords easy.

Review where you are logged in

Check active sessions and linked devices regularly, and remove any you do not recognize.

Watch for phishing

Be careful with links and messages that claim your account will be disabled. Never share codes.
