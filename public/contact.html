Contact FAQ

Before you message

How fast will I get a reply?

The AI assistant answers instantly. Human team members reply when someone is online, so it can take longer outside team hours.

Do I need to attach screenshots?

They are not required, but they help. Notices, error messages and dates let us review your case more accurately.

Will you ask for my password?

Never. We do not ask for passwords, login codes or ID documents in chat.

Can you help with an account that is not mine?

No. We only assist the rightful owner of an account or page.
